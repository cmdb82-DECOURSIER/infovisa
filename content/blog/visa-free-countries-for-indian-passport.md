# Visa-Free Countries for Indian Passport Holders in 2025

The Indian passport has steadily improved its global standing. As of 2025, Indian passport holders can visit over 60 destinations visa-free or with visa-on-arrival. Here's your complete guide.

## Visa-Free Destinations (No Visa Required)

These countries allow Indian passport holders to enter without any prior visa:

**Asia**
- Bhutan — unlimited stay for Indians (special treaty)
- Nepal — unlimited stay for Indians (special treaty)  
- Indonesia — 30 days free (new policy from 2024)
- Thailand — 30 days free (new policy from 2024)
- Sri Lanka — 30 days via ETA (free for Indian citizens)
- Maldives — 30 days on arrival (free)
- Macau — 30 days
- Hong Kong — 14 days

**Africa**
- Kenya — eVisa (easy online process)
- Tanzania — eVisa
- Rwanda — eVisa or on arrival
- Senegal — 90 days
- Mauritius — 90 days
- Seychelles — 30 days

**Americas**
- Ecuador — 90 days (no visa)
- Haiti — 90 days
- Trinidad and Tobago — with prior OCI/PIO card

**Caribbean**
- Barbados — 6 months
- Jamaica — 30 days
- Dominican Republic — 30 days (tourist card required)

## Visa on Arrival

These countries issue visas at the airport upon arrival:

**Asia & Pacific**
- Cambodia — $30, 30 days
- Myanmar — $50, 28 days
- Timor-Leste — $30, 30 days
- Tuvalu — 30 days
- Samoa — 60 days

**Middle East**
- Jordan — JD 40, 30 days
- Qatar — Free, 30 days (from 2023)
- Bahrain — 14 days (BHD 5)

**Africa**
- Ethiopia — $52, 30 days
- Madagascar — Free, 90 days
- Mozambique — $50, 30 days
- Uganda — $50, 30 days

## Easy eVisa Countries (Apply Online)

These countries offer simple online applications, typically approved within 24-72 hours:

- Turkey — €50, 30 days
- Egypt — $25, 30 days  
- India's neighbors: No eVisa needed
- Vietnam — Free e-visa, 90 days (new 2023 policy)
- Malaysia — Free eNTRI for 15 days

## Countries Requiring Advance Visa

For aspirational travelers, here are some of the most common visa requirements:

**United States** — B-2 Tourist Visa: $185, processing 6-18 months, 10-year multiple entry once approved

**United Kingdom** — Standard Visitor Visa: £115, 15-21 working days, valid up to 10 years

**Schengen Area** — €80, 15 working days, up to 90 days/180 days

**Canada** — $185 CAD, 4-8 weeks, sometimes 6+ months

**Australia** — Tourist visa AUD 190, 4-8 weeks

## Tips for Indian Travelers

**Get the right documents ready:** For any visa application, having 6+ months of bank statements showing a healthy balance (ideally ₹3-5 lakh minimum) dramatically improves success rates.

**Book refundable travel:** Never book non-refundable flights before your visa is confirmed.

**Start early for US/UK:** These visas can take 6-18 months. Start planning a year in advance.

**Use a strong financial profile:** Government employees, IT professionals, and business owners statistically have higher visa approval rates.

**Consider OCI card benefits:** Overseas Citizens of India get significantly better access to many countries.

## Improving Your Passport Power

Travel history matters. Having stamps from Japan, Schengen, UK, or USA in your passport makes subsequent applications much easier, as it demonstrates you return home from international trips.
