# The 10 Fastest & Easiest Visas to Get in 2025

Sometimes you need to travel fast. Here are the 10 destinations with the simplest, quickest visa processes available to most nationalities.

## 1. Turkey — eVisa (5 minutes, $50)

Turkey's eVisa system is a model of efficiency. Most nationalities can apply online, receive approval within minutes, and pay with a credit card. The visa is valid for 180 days with 30 or 90 days of stay depending on nationality.

**Apply at:** evisa.gov.tr
**Processing:** 5 minutes to 24 hours
**Cost:** $50-75 depending on nationality
**Validity:** 180 days, multiple entry

## 2. Kenya — eVisa (48 hours, $51)

Kenya's revamped eVisa system (launched 2023) makes getting into this East African gem incredibly straightforward. Fill out the form, upload documents, pay online, receive your visa by email.

**Apply at:** evisa.go.ke
**Processing:** 1-3 business days
**Cost:** $51 USD
**Validity:** 90 days, single entry

## 3. Cambodia — Visa on Arrival ($30, 5 minutes at airport)

Cambodia makes it easy at Phnom Penh and Siem Reap airports. Pay $30, hand over a passport photo and your passport, and you're through in minutes. You can also apply for an e-Visa online beforehand.

**Processing:** Immediate (on arrival) or 3 days (e-Visa)
**Cost:** $30
**Stay:** 30 days, extendable once

## 4. Georgia — Free, Visa on Arrival (Most nationalities)

Most passport holders get 365 days in Georgia visa-free. That's not a typo — 365 days. The country actively woos digital nomads and tourists with one of the world's most generous visa policies.

**Cost:** Free
**Stay:** 365 days
**Ease:** Just show up

## 5. Sri Lanka — ETA (24 hours, Free for some)

Sri Lanka's Electronic Travel Authorization is a quick online process. For Indian citizens it's free; for most others it costs $20. The country's tourism revival has made the process very smooth.

**Apply at:** eta.gov.lk
**Processing:** 24 hours
**Cost:** Free (Indians) or $20
**Stay:** 30 days, extendable to 90

## 6. Vietnam — E-Visa (90 days, $25)

Vietnam dramatically improved its visa policy in 2023. Most nationalities now get a 90-day single or multiple-entry visa for $25, approved within 3 business days online.

**Apply at:** evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn
**Processing:** 3 business days
**Cost:** $25
**Stay:** 90 days

## 7. Maldives — Free Visa on Arrival (30 days)

The Maldives gives everyone a free 30-day tourist visa on arrival. Simply book your resort and show up. Easy as it gets.

**Cost:** Free
**Stay:** 30 days (extendable)
**Requirements:** Hotel booking + return ticket

## 8. Morocco — Visa on Arrival for Most (Free, 90 days)

Many nationalities including the US, EU, UK, Canada, and Australia can enter Morocco without a visa for up to 90 days. Just show up at the airport or land border.

**Cost:** Free
**Stay:** 90 days
**Requirements:** Passport valid 6+ months

## 9. Mauritius — Free, 60 days on Arrival

This island paradise off East Africa grants 60 days free to most nationalities. Extendable for a modest fee. One of the most welcoming visa policies in Africa.

**Cost:** Free
**Stay:** 60 days (extendable to 6 months)

## 10. Albania — Free, Seasonal Policy

During summer months, Albania opens its borders to most nationalities without any visa requirement, making it one of Europe's most accessible non-Schengen destinations.

**Cost:** Free (seasonal)
**Stay:** 90 days

## Tips for Fast Travel

**Always check current policies:** Visa policies can change with little notice. The list above is accurate for early 2025 but may change.

**Bring passport photos:** Many on-arrival systems require 1-2 physical passport photos. Carry a few in your travel wallet.

**Have proof of onward travel:** Most countries technically require a return or onward ticket. Frontier staff check this.

**Carry enough cash:** On-arrival fees are often cash-only. Carry USD or EUR as fallback currencies.
