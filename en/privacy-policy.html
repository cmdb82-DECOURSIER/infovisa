Privacy policy

How InfoVisa.lu collects and uses data. We are committed to GDPR compliance.

Page updated on 04 May 2026 — sources: European Commission, EU Council, national consulates.

Who we are

InfoVisa.lu is an independent informational website operated from Luxembourg. Contact: contact@infovisa.lu.

Data we collect

  • Server logs — IP address, browser, pages visited (kept 30 days). Used for security and aggregate analytics only.
  • Cookies — preference cookies (your accepted language and consent), and Google AdSense cookies if you accept them.
  • Contact form — name and email if you write to us. Used only to reply.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display ads. AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can control personalisation:

  • If you click "Accept" on the cookie banner, AdSense may serve personalised ads.
  • If you click "Reject non-essential", we tell AdSense to serve only non-personalised ads.
  • You can manage Google ad settings at any time at adssettings.google.com.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you live in the EU/EEA, you have the right to: access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to, and port your personal data. Contact us at contact@infovisa.lu to exercise any right. You can also lodge a complaint with the Luxembourg data protection authority (CNPD) at cnpd.public.lu.

Data we do not collect

We do not ask for, store or process passport data, application files, or any personal documents you may discuss in correspondence. Only the consulate handles your visa file.

Third parties

  • Google AdSense — for advertising. Privacy policy.
  • Hosting provider — based in the EU, GDPR-compliant.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.
